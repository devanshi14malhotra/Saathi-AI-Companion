# Merchant Settlement Basics

Merchant payments collected via QR and Soundbox are settled to your linked bank account according to your settlement cycle.

Steps:
1. Standard settlements run on T+1 working days. Bank holidays and weekends shift settlements to the next working day automatically.
2. Check your Merchant Dashboard -> Settlements to view expected amount, deductions, and UTR reference numbers.
3. If a settlement is held for KYC or risk review, or shows an amount mismatch, contact support for reconciliation.

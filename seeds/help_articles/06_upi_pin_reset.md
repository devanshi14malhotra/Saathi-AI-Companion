# How to Reset Your UPI PIN

If you forgot your UPI PIN or entered an incorrect PIN too many times, you can reset it anytime.

Steps:
1. Go to Profile -> UPI & Payment Settings and select your linked bank account.
2. Tap Change or Reset UPI PIN next to the account name.
3. Enter the last 6 digits and expiry date of your debit card (or select Aadhaar verification if supported by your bank).
4. Enter the bank OTP received via SMS and set your new UPI PIN. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.

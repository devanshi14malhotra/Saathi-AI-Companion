# App Crash and Freezing Troubleshooting

If the app crashes, hangs, or fails to open on your device, follow these troubleshooting steps while we log an engineering ticket with your device model and OS version.

Steps:
1. Clear the app cache from Settings -> Apps -> Storage -> Clear Cache and restart your phone.
2. Update the app to the latest version from Play Store / App Store and ensure at least 500 MB of free storage space.
3. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data and disable any third-party screen overlay or VPN apps.

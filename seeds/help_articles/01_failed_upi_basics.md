# Failed UPI Payment Basics

If money was deducted from your bank account for a failed UPI or bill payment, do not worry. Under banking guidelines, failed UPI debits are automatically reversed by your issuing or acquiring bank within 24 hours (T+1), and merchant bill payments within 72 hours.

Steps:
1. Check your transaction status in Payment History before retrying.
2. Wait 24 hours for automatic bank reversal on pending UPI transactions.
3. If the payment shows Failed and Debited past the reversal window, Saathi automatically initiates a verified refund to your source account.

# Adding a Bank Account for UPI

To link a bank account for UPI payments, ensure your active mobile SIM card matches the mobile number registered with your bank.

Steps:
1. Tap your profile icon on the top left and select UPI & Payment Settings.
2. Tap Add Another Bank Account and select your bank from the list.
3. The app will verify your mobile number via an encrypted SMS and link your account automatically.
4. Set a 4 or 6 digit UPI PIN using your debit card or Aadhaar OTP verification if linking for the first time.

# Completing Account and Merchant KYC

Completing KYC unlocks full wallet limits and uninterrupted merchant settlements.

Steps:
1. Open Profile -> KYC Status and select Upgrade / Complete KYC.
2. Keep your original PAN card and Aadhaar number ready for Video KYC verification.
3. Enter your PAN and Aadhaar details, verify the UIDAI OTP, and start a short video call with an authorized verification agent in a well-lit room.
4. Once approved, KYC status updates within 24 hours.

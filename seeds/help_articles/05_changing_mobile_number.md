# Changing Registered Mobile Number

If you have changed your mobile number, you can update it on your account while keeping your payment history intact.

Steps:
1. Open Profile Settings and tap on your registered mobile number.
2. Select Update Mobile Number and verify ownership using the OTP sent to your existing number or via KYC verification.
3. Enter your new mobile number, verify the SMS OTP, and re-link your UPI bank accounts using the new SIM card.

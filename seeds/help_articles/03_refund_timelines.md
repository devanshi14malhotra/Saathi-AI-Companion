# Refund Timelines and Tracking

Once a refund (RF-xxxx) is initiated for a failed or cancelled order, the money is credited back to the original payment source.

Steps:
1. UPI payments: Refunds typically reflect within 24 to 72 hours.
2. Utility bill payments and recharges: Refunds take up to 72 hours (3 working days).
3. If your refund remains in PROCESSING status for more than 72 hours, share the transaction ID or refund ID here so we can raise a bank reconciliation (RC-xxxx).

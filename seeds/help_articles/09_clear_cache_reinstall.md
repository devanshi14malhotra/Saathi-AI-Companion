# Clearing App Cache and Reinstalling

If screens load slowly or balances do not refresh, clearing the app cache resolves stale local data.

Steps:
1. Long-press the app icon on your phone and open App Info -> Storage & Cache.
2. Tap Clear Cache (do not tap Clear Storage unless you are ready to log in again).
3. Force stop and reopen the app. If the issue persists, reinstall the latest version from the official store.

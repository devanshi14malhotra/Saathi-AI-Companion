# Changing App Language Settings

You can use the app and customer support in English, Hindi, Hinglish, and regional Indian languages.

Steps:
1. Tap the Profile icon on the top left and select Choose Language.
2. Select your preferred language (English, Hindi, etc.) and tap Apply.
3. In Saathi customer support chat, you can type in English, Hindi, or Hinglish at any time and receive replies in the same language.

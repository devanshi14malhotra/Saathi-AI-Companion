# QR Code and Soundbox Troubleshooting for Merchants

If your Soundbox is not announcing payments or QR scans fail, follow these quick checks.

Steps:
1. Press the power and volume buttons on your Soundbox to verify battery charge and 4G network signal.
2. Press the replay button to hear the last transaction announcement and verify language settings in the Merchant App.
3. Ensure your printed QR sticker is clean and linked to an active bank account in Merchant Settings.

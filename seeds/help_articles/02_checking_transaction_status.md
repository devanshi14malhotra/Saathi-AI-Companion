# Checking Transaction Status

You can check the real-time status of any UPI, wallet, or bill payment directly inside the app.

Steps:
1. Open the Balance & History section from the home screen.
2. Tap on the specific transaction to view the Transaction ID (TXN-xxxx), bank reference number (RRN), and current debit status.
3. Look at the three checkpoints: Payment Initiated, Bank Debited, and Merchant Credited. If a transaction is Pending, the bank will confirm final status shortly.

# Safe Payments and Login OTP Help

Protect your account from fraud and resolve OTP delivery issues safely.

Steps:
1. If you are not receiving a login OTP, check network signal, ensure SMS inbox is not full, wait 60 seconds, and tap Resend OTP.
2. Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, or password with anyone—even if they claim to be customer support or bank staff.
3. Never install screen-sharing apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) when making payments. Remember: entering a UPI PIN is only required to SEND money, never to receive a refund.
